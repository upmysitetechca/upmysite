Website basics: a practical checklist for business owners.

A website should explain what you do, build confidence and make it easy for the right customer to contact you.

Five foundations to get right

1. A clear message

State who you help, what you provide and where you work. Avoid making visitors guess what your business does.

2. Useful page structure

Most small businesses need a clear home page, service pages, an about page, contact details and helpful supporting content.

3. Mobile usability

Check that text is readable, navigation works, buttons are easy to tap and forms are simple on smaller screens.

4. Trust and proof

Use genuine reviews with permission, accurate credentials, real project examples and clear contact information.

5. Ongoing care

Plan for software updates, backups, security monitoring, broken-link checks and content improvements.

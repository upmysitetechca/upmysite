Burlington Website Maintenance: A Practical Guide for Small Businesses

What does website maintenance include?

Website maintenance is the ongoing work that keeps a site functioning and current. It can include content edits, software and plugin updates, backups, security checks, uptime monitoring, broken-link reviews and performance improvements.

Why local businesses should plan for it

A broken enquiry form, outdated opening hours or a security warning can undermine trust. Regular checks help catch small issues before they become bigger interruptions. Maintenance also gives a business a planned way to update seasonal offers, new services, team information and project galleries.

A practical monthly checklist

Review the website on a phone and desktop; test contact forms and key buttons; check business hours and service details; update the CMS, themes and plugins after taking a backup; confirm backups can be restored; review security and uptime alerts; scan important pages for broken links; and check analytics for unusual changes.

How to choose a maintenance provider

Ask which platforms they support, what is included each month, how requests are submitted, whether backups are taken before changes, how urgent issues are handled and who owns the website and its accounts. Get the scope, exclusions, response expectations and price in writing.

Maintenance versus a one-off fix

A recurring plan is useful when updates, monitoring and small changes are ongoing. A one-time job may be more suitable for a specific issue such as malware cleanup, a migration or a speed problem. Ask for a clear diagnosis before agreeing to either approach.

Questions to ask before signing

What exactly is included? How many content-edit hours are available? Do unused hours roll over? Are emergency fixes extra? How frequently are backups made? Can you access and export your site? What happens if you cancel? Written answers make providers easier to compare.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a small business website be checked?

Check key forms, contact details and important pages regularly. Software updates and security checks should follow a consistent schedule appropriate to the platform and risk.

Does website maintenance help SEO?

It can support technical health by addressing broken links, availability, page performance and outdated content. Maintenance alone does not guarantee rankings.

Can I maintain my own website?

Yes, if you have the time and skills to manage updates, backups, security and troubleshooting. Keep reliable backups and test changes carefully.
